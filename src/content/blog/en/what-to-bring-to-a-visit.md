## Clothes you can sit in for an hour

Choose loose clothes without tight belts or collars, that you can sit in for an hour or two in an armchair. The room is warm, but you can take a blanket if you like.

## Something to read or listen to

A book, headphones with your music, or a podcast. Some people prefer to bring nothing and sit with their eyes closed.

## Water

Bring a bottle of water or take one from reception.

## What you can leave at home

The laptop and the diary. Your phone can stay with you, on silent.
