There is no required preparation for a session. But after hundreds of visits we have noticed a few small things that make the difference between an hour "ticked off" and an hour that is genuinely restful.

## Clothes you can sit still in

Choose loose clothes without tight belts or collars, that you can sit in for an hour or two without fidgeting. The room is warm, but you can take a blanket if you want one. Shoes can stay at the entrance.

## Your phone: silent or in the locker

The easiest way to spoil an hour of quiet is a notification. Leave your phone in a locker at the cloakroom, or switch it to silent before you go in. If you are expecting an important call, tell us at reception and we will let you know.

## Water, not coffee

Bring a bottle of water or take one from reception. A coffee right before a session can leave you restless exactly when you want to unwind. A heavy meal just before does not help either; a light snack an hour earlier is plenty.

## What to bring for yourself

A book, headphones with your favourite music, or a quiet podcast. Some clients prefer to bring nothing and sit with their eyes closed. There is no right answer.

## How much time to set aside

Arrive ten minutes early, and when you leave give yourself a few minutes of transition before going back into traffic or the office. If you can, avoid scheduling anything pressing straight afterwards.

## A note on context

Everything here is about comfort and enjoying your time at the centre. Sessions are relaxation experiences, not a medical service. For any question about your health, talk to your doctor.
