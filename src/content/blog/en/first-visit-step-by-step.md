Many people who write to us before their first visit ask the same thing: "What do I actually have to do?" The short answer is: almost nothing. Here is how a first visit goes, from arrival to departure.

## Before you arrive

Book a 60- or 90-minute EESystem session on WhatsApp, on your own or for a small group, from the "Book on WhatsApp" button. We confirm the day and time in the same conversation. Come in comfortable clothes and, if you can, ten minutes before your booked time.

## Arrival and introduction

At reception you leave your coat and bags, and a member of the team shows you around: the session room, the armchairs, the loungers, where to find water and blankets, the toilet. They answer your questions, then let you settle in. The introduction takes a few minutes and happens only once, on your first visit.

## During the visit

The session room is equipped with the EESystem. You settle in, clothed, in an armchair, on a lounger or on the sofa, choose your position and, if you like, a blanket. The light is soft and the background sound is discreet. From here on, the time is yours: you can read, listen to something on headphones, or close your eyes. Nobody touches you and nothing is asked of you. If you want to leave early, open the door or press the call button.

## When you leave

At the end, someone from the team lets you know discreetly that your slot is over. You can sit for a few minutes in the waiting area with a glass of water before you go. You pay at reception, by card or in cash, and receive a fiscal receipt.
