We have no contact form and no booking platform. Every booking is a WhatsApp conversation, because it is the fastest way to agree on a time and answer questions in the same place.

## Step 1: write to us

Tap the "Book on WhatsApp" button on any page of the site. WhatsApp opens with a ready-made message: a greeting and the intention to book. If you tapped the button on a specific service, the message already names that service. Add your first name and one or two options for the day and time, then send.

If you do not use WhatsApp, you can call the number on the contact page during opening hours.

## Step 2: we confirm the time

We reply during opening hours, usually the same day. If the slot you want is free, we confirm it straight away; if not, we suggest the closest alternatives. Our message is the confirmation: there is no email and nothing to fill in. TODO(content): confirm the usual response time.

## Step 3: come to the centre

Come at the agreed time, about ten minutes early on your first visit. You pay at reception, by card or in cash. If you need to cancel or move the booking, write to us in the same conversation at least 24 hours in advance.

## What happens to your messages

The conversation stays in WhatsApp, on your phone and on ours. The site sends nothing anywhere until you tap "Send", sets no cookies and stores no messages. The details are in the privacy policy.
