When choosing a relaxation centre, a few simple questions tell you more than any brochure.

## About the space

- Can I see the room before I book?
- How many people are in the room at the same time?
- Can I leave at any point during the session?

## About promises

- What does the centre say happens in a session? If the answer contains health promises, take it as a warning sign.
- How are other clients' experiences presented: as personal accounts or as guaranteed results?
- Do the documents on display have authors, a year and an evidence type?

## About money and data

- What is the cancellation policy?
- What personal data is collected, and why?
- Do I get a receipt or an invoice?
