Many people who write to us before their first visit ask the same thing: "What do I actually have to do?" The short answer is: almost nothing. Here is how a first session goes, from arrival to departure.

## Before you arrive

Book a 60- or 120-minute slot from the booking page. You will receive a confirmation by e-mail with the address, a map link and a few suggestions. Come in comfortable clothes and, if you can, ten minutes before your booked time.

## Arrival and introduction

At reception you leave your coat and bags, and a member of the team shows you around: the session room, the armchairs, where to find water and blankets, the toilet. They briefly explain the equipment and what you will see and hear in the room, then answer your questions. The introduction takes five to ten minutes and happens only once, on your first visit.

## During the session

You settle into an armchair, choose your position and, if you like, a blanket. The light is soft and the background sound is discreet. From here on, the time is yours: you can read, listen to something on headphones, or close your eyes. Nobody touches you and nothing is asked of you. If you want to leave early, open the door or press the call button.

## After the session

At the end, someone from the team lets you know discreetly that your slot is over. You can sit for a few minutes in the waiting area with a glass of water before you go. If you like, tell us how it felt; client-reported experiences help us improve the space, but they are not, and will not be, presented as results.

## One important thing

Sessions are relaxation and wellness experiences. They are not a medical service, they do not replace a consultation, and they make no claim to change anything about your health. If you have a question about your health, ask your doctor before booking.
