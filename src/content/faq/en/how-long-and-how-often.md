Standard sessions last 60 or 120 minutes, and the slot starts at the time you booked. Please arrive about ten minutes early, especially on your first visit, so there is time for the introduction.

There is no "right" frequency. Some clients come once a month, as a scheduled pause; others prefer a few visits close together and then a break. Session packages are designed for people who want to come back at their own pace without booking each time.

If you have questions about your health, talk to your doctor before booking. We do not give medical advice.
