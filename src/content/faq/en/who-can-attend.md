Sessions are open to adults. Minors may attend only when accompanied by a parent or legal guardian who stays in the centre for the whole visit and signs the consent form at reception.

If you are pregnant, wear a medical device or have any question about your health, please talk to your doctor before booking. We cannot assess such situations ourselves and we do not give medical advice.

You can end your session at any time: simply open the door or press the call button in the room, and someone from the team will come straight away.
