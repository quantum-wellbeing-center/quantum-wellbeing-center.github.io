category: booking

Your first name, the service you want (for example the 60-minute session) and one or two options for the day and time. That is all.

If you have booked with us before, just tell us the day and time. For packages, mention that you want to use a session from your package.

We do not ask for anything else and we do not keep messages beyond what the booking needs. The details are in the privacy policy.
