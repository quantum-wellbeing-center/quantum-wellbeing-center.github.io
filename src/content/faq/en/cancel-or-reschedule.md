Yes. Message us on WhatsApp at least 24 hours before your booked time and we move or cancel the slot at no cost.

For cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice, or if you do not turn up, the session counts as used when it is part of a package. If something unexpected came up, tell us: we will find a solution together.
