Yes. You can cancel or move a booking free of charge at least 24 hours before the scheduled time, using the link in your confirmation e-mail or by messaging us on WhatsApp.

For cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice, or if you do not turn up, the session counts as used when it is part of a package. We know unexpected things happen, so write to us: we will find a solution together.
