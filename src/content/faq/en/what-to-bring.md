You do not need anything special. Come in comfortable clothes you can relax in for an hour or two. The room is warm, and you can take a blanket at the entrance if you like.

Feel free to bring a book, headphones and a bottle of water. You may keep your phone with you, but we suggest switching it to silent so it does not interrupt your quiet or anyone else's.
