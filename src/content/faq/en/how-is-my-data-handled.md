We collect only what we need for a booking: your name, a phone number and, if you book online, your e-mail address. We do not ask for or store information about your health.

Your data is used to confirm and manage appointments and is not sold or passed to third parties for marketing. The booking tool we use has its own privacy policy, linked from the booking form.
