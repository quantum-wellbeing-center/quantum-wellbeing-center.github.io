A session is a block of time you spend in a quiet, softly lit room, seated in a comfortable armchair. There is nothing you have to do: you can read, listen to music on headphones, or simply sit with your eyes closed.

On your first visit, a member of the team shows you the space, explains how the equipment works and answers your questions. Then we leave you in peace until the end of your booked slot. Nobody touches you and nothing is asked of you.

Sessions are relaxation and wellness experiences. They are not a medical service and do not replace a consultation, a diagnosis or a treatment prescribed by your doctor.
