EESystem sessions last 60 or 90 minutes, and the slot starts at the time you booked. On your first visit, come about ten minutes early so there is time to show you around.

You can leave before the end at any time: open the door or press the call button in the room.

How often you come is up to you. The 3 + 1 package is for people who want to come back: you pay for three 60-minute sessions and the fourth is free.
