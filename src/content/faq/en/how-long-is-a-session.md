Sessions last 60 or 120 minutes, and the slot starts at the time you booked. On your first visit, come about ten minutes early so there is time to show you around.

You can leave before the end at any time: open the door or press the call button in the room.

How often you come is up to you. The five-session package is for people who want to come back without paying each time.
