No. Our sessions are relaxation and wellness experiences, not medical services. We do not diagnose, we do not give medical recommendations, and we do not claim that sessions have any effect on any condition.

What you find on this site, including the "Testimonials" and "Research" sections, are client-reported experiences and documents labelled by their type of evidence. Do not read them as promises of a result.

If you are receiving treatment or have a question about your health, talk to your doctor before booking, and never stop a prescribed treatment because of anything you read here.
