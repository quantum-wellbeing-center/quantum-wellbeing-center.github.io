You arrive at reception, leave your coat and bags, and a member of the team takes you to the session room. On your first visit they show you the space and answer your questions; it takes a few minutes.

You settle into an armchair, take a blanket if you like, and your slot begins. You can read, listen to something on headphones or simply sit. Nobody touches you and nothing is asked of you. If you want to leave early, open the door or press the call button.
