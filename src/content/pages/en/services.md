You arrive at reception, leave your coat and bags, and a member of the team takes you to the session room. On your first visit they show you the space and answer your questions; it takes a few minutes.

The session room is equipped with the EESystem. You settle in, clothed, in an armchair, on a lounger or on the sofa, take a blanket if you like, and your slot begins: 60 or 90 minutes, depending on the service you chose. You can read, listen to something on headphones or simply sit with your eyes closed. Nobody touches you and nothing is asked of you. If you want to leave early, open the door or press the call button.
