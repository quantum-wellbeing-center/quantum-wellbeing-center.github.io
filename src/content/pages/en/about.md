Born to genius parents, both leading scientists in the field of physical engineering, Dr. Michael has taught holistic health for more than four decades, inventing the first “scalar light healing chamber” in 1978, in her “Health Salon”. Her contributions to health have highlighted the importance of holistic medicine in the modern approach to healing.

Dr. Sandra Rose Michael’s work in applied integrative biophysics, which has won many awards over recent decades, is recognised and valued by prestigious institutions such as Presidential and International Who’s Who.

Dr. Michael has lectured at the United Nations, MIT, the World Health Summit, the Harvard Club, London’s Royal Society of Medicine, the Mount Sinai School of Medicine, the Scalar Research Experts Conference, Tesla Tech, congresses on Regenerative and Anti-Aging Technology, and at numerous medical schools and Ministries of Health around the world.

Dr. Michael was awarded a knighthood for her humanitarian service and has served on the board of directors of the Global Foundation for Integrative Medicine. She has also served as a Commissioner for the International Tribunal for Natural Justice.
