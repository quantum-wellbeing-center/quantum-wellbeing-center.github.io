The library gathers documents related to the kind of space and equipment we use. We publish them so you can read them yourself, not to back any claim about effects. Two rules guide us: every document carries an honest label of its type of evidence, and no testimonial or pilot report is ever presented as a clinical study.

Read each document with its label in mind. A pilot study on a handful of people says something different from a peer-reviewed paper, and a personal account is not evidence.
