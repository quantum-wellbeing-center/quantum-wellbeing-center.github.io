## What a session is

A session is a 60 or 120-minute slot that you spend in a quiet room fitted with our technology, in a comfortable armchair. It is a relaxation and wellbeing experience: there is no protocol to follow, no touch involved and nothing you have to do. ## The room

The session room has soft lighting, a constant temperature and a discreet background sound. The armchairs are spaced apart, and blankets and water are within reach. The equipment is installed around the room and runs quietly; you will not see or hear more than a faint hum. TODO(content): confirm the number of seats and the details of the room.

## How long it lasts

You can choose a 60 or a 120-minute slot. On your first visit, add ten minutes for a short introduction to the space. There is no "correct" frequency: some clients come once a month, others weekly. Pick the rhythm that suits you and your budget.

## What to expect

You come in comfortable clothes, leave your things at reception and sit down. You can read, listen to something on headphones or simply close your eyes. If you want to leave early, you open the door or press the call button. At the end, someone from the team lets you know discreetly that the slot is over, and you can stay a few more minutes in the waiting area.

## What a session is not

A session is not a medical act. We do not diagnose, we do not give medical advice and we do not claim that sessions have any effect on any condition. Sessions do not replace a consultation, a diagnosis or a treatment set by a doctor. If you have a question about your health or are following a treatment, talk to your doctor before booking, and never stop a prescribed treatment because of anything you read here.
